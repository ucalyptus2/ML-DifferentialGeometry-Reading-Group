The big ideas

Manifolds

Data (poses, covariances, shapes, hierarchies) lives on curved spaces, not flat ℝⁿ.

Tangent spaces

Every update vector & gradient lives in TₓM — the linear window onto the manifold.

Geodesics & exp/log

Shortest paths and local coordinates; the heart of Riemannian optimization.

Groups & equivariance

f(g·x)=g·f(x): build invariance to rotation, reflection, translation, E(n).

Gauges & transport

Align local frames with connections to handle vector/tensor fields correctly.

Curvature

Hyperbolic (−κ) fits trees; spherical (+κ) fits rotations. Geometry is a design choice.
